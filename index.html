About

Hi! I'm Shadi, a graduate student in Computer Science at the University of Maine, where I'm working toward my M.S. degree. My research interests include privacy, trustworthy machine learning, explainable AI, and human-centered computing. My research experience includes user studies of web tracking and IoT privacy, as well as investigating privacy risks and explainability in machine learning.

Before graduate school, I worked as a data scientist on financial analytics, fraud detection, and data-driven systems. I earned my B.Sc. in Applied Mathematics, with a minor in Computer Science, from Amirkabir University of Technology in Tehran.

Selected Projects

FALL
2026
Membership Inference and the Evaluation Crisis: A Literature Survey
Privacy in Machine Learning · University of Maine

Surveys membership inference attacks, from shadow-model constructions to likelihood-ratio methods, and recent critiques showing that many reported results against LLMs reflect differences between member and non-member data rather than true membership. Proposes an evaluation protocol in which attacks must beat a blind, model-free baseline and report the true-positive rate at a low false-positive rate.
